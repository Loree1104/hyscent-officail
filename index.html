ABOUT HYSCENT

The Story Behind The Scent

"Fragrance is not simply something you wear. It is a memory, an emotion and a story that remains long after you leave."

HYSCENT is a luxury fragrance house dedicated to creating original niche perfumes that combine oriental inspiration, natural ingredients and contemporary aesthetics. We transform memories, landscapes and emotions into unique olfactory experiences.
